Come along to meet the ScotlandIS team and hear what support you can tap into as an early-stage Techscaler member, free for the first year!

[https://www.techscaler.co.uk](https://www.techscaler.co.uk)

The Improvement Service (IS) is a national organization for local government improvement in Scotland, established by COSLA and SOLACE to help councils and their partners enhance local services, governance, and community leadership. It provides support, including data and research, technology platforms, and programs, to enable local authorities to achieve efficiencies, reduce inequality, and deliver high-quality, effective services to the public.

It is also a representative of Scotland’s digital technologies industry, with paid memberships for businesses of all sizes. It offers businesses benefits such as networking opportunities, enhanced visibility, exclusive research, and a range of discounts on business essentials like insurance, software, and events. Members also get access to funding calls, procurement opportunities, and business support services like HR and legal advice.
